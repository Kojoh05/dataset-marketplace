Data science project ideas, with the datasets to build them

Most data science portfolios use the same toy datasets. These project ideas use realistic business data with the problems real teams face: leaky columns, selection bias, censored demand and time-based validation. Each one has a ready dataset on KOJOH.

1. Onboarding funnel analysis

Free · Beginner · Python, SQL, Statistics

A free product analytics project: an onboarding funnel and an A/B test where the dashboard says the new screen lost. Find out what really happened.

You will practise: A/B test analysis with real pitfalls; Event data cleaning; Conversion by platform and source; Experiment readout for a product manager.

Interview question it prepares you for: What would you check before trusting an A/B test result?

See the project: The Funnel That Leaked: App Onboarding and an A/B Test

2. Customer churn prediction

₹249 · Hard · Python, SQL, Machine Learning, Power BI

Predict who leaves a streaming app in the next 90 days from four messy exports, without falling for the leaky columns, and turn it into a call list worth rupees.

You will practise: Leak-free feature engineering; Voluntary vs involuntary churn; Time-based model validation; Turning model scores into a business call list.

Interview question it prepares you for: How do you make sure a churn model has no data leakage?

See the project: The Quiet Quitters: Streaming App Churn Prediction, India

3. Demand forecasting with stockouts

₹249 · Hard · Python, SQL, Machine Learning, Power BI

Forecast demand for a ten minute grocery app when sales hide demand: 92,000 days of store x SKU data where Saturday looks slow only because the shelves are empty.

You will practise: Censored demand estimation; Festival, promo and event effects; Forecast accuracy (WAPE) vs a baseline; Inventory planning recommendations.

Interview question it prepares you for: Why is sales data not the same as demand, and how did you adjust for it?

See the project: Stockout Saturdays: Quick Commerce Demand Forecasting, India

4. Credit scoring and default prediction

₹449 · Hard · Python, SQL, Machine Learning, Statistics

Build a credit scoring model for an Indian NBFC where only approved loans have outcomes, recent loans have not had time to default and the loan file leaks the answer.

You will practise: Selection bias and reject inference; Vintage analysis; Model calibration; Approval cut-off with profit economics.

Interview question it prepares you for: What is selection bias in credit data and how does it affect your model?

See the project: Approved Only: NBFC Personal Loan Credit Scoring, India

How to make any project stand out

  • Always compare your model with a simple baseline.
  • Split by time, not at random, when the business predicts the future.
  • Check every feature for leakage before training.
  • Translate model quality into rupees or decisions.

Questions

Where can I find datasets for these projects?

Each idea on this page links to a KOJOH project with its own messy dataset, tasks and a data dictionary. Kaggle and government open data portals are other good free sources.

Are these projects good for a portfolio?

Yes. Each one ends with cleaned data, answered business questions, a dashboard and a one page recommendation, which is what recruiters look for. Paid KOJOH projects give every buyer a personal brief, so your answers are your own.

Is the data real?

No. Every KOJOH dataset is synthetic, built to behave like real Indian business data, including the mess.
