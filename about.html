About KOJOH

KOJOH is a dataset marketplace built for people who want a data career. Not another folder of clean CSVs, but business problems with the messy data that comes with them.

Why we exist

Most learning datasets are toy problems. Employers hire for judgment on real, ambiguous data. Every KOJOH dataset is inspired by the kind of problems teams in Logistics, Sales, Service, Product, E-commerce and HR deal with. Every row is synthetic: the companies, people and transactions are invented. Each dataset has its own schema, its own story and a challenge worth solving.

What makes it different

Datasets are organized by job track (Data Analyst, Data Scientist, Business / Ops, Data Engineer, Product Analyst and Quality Analyst) rather than generic topic labels. Every dataset keeps its own independent schema, because real business data never looks the same twice. Search is powered by a transparent ontology of job titles, tools, and domains, not an AI black box.

Built by

KOJOH is built and maintained by Yash Chauhan, a data analyst who builds data systems for Sales, Logistics, Service and E-commerce teams. KOJOH is the practice he wanted when he started: messy data, a real question, and no answer key in the next tab.
