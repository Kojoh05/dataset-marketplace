What KOJOH does

Real, independent datasets

Every dataset keeps its own schema instead of being forced into one generic table, because real business data never looks the same twice.

Organized by job track

Browse by the role you're building toward, not just a keyword search. Each folder is a career path, not a category label.

A challenge, not just a file

Each dataset comes with the business context behind it (what question it's meant to answer), so your portfolio shows judgment, not just SQL.

Search that understands the job

Type a role, a tool, or a domain and get matches, powered by an ontology of job titles, skills, and synonyms, not a black-box AI.

Built for four job tracks

Pick the track you're aiming for at signup. It's how your recommendations get tailored.

Data Analyst

Fraud detection, churn, quotation drop-off, the highest-demand track for job-seekers.

Data Engineer

Messy multi-source ETL, slowly-changing dimensions, and broken referential integrity to untangle.

Product Analyst

Ambiguous A/B results, drop-off funnels, and feature-adoption questions with no clean answer.

Quality Analyst

Inconsistent defect logs and process data that needs structure before it can be trusted.
