Project ideas

Data analyst project ideas, with the datasets to build them

A good data analyst project starts with a business question, uses messy data, and ends with a recommendation someone could act on. Here are project ideas that do exactly that, each with a ready dataset on KOJOH so you can start today instead of hunting for data. They are ordered from beginner to advanced.

Start with a free projectCheck your level first

1. Retail sales analysis for a small shop

Free · Beginner · Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI

A free first project: a year of real-looking bills from a Lucknow kirana store. Clean it, find what makes money and how much is stuck in udhaar.

You will practise: Profit vs revenue by product; Customer credit (udhaar) tracking; Busiest hours and staffing analysis; Festival (Diwali) sales impact.

Interview question it prepares you for: Which products sell a lot but make little profit, and how did you find them?

See the project: Kirana Store Sales: A Lucknow General Store's Year of Bills

2. Small business sales analysis in Excel

Free · Beginner · Excel, Power BI

A free Excel and Power BI project: a year of a Pune home tiffin service's order sheet, typed by three people. Clean it, find what makes money and chase the dues.

You will practise: Customer dues and collections tracker; Price increase impact analysis; Area and plan profitability; Power BI dashboard for a small business.

Interview question it prepares you for: How did you clean dates typed in four different styles in Excel?

See the project: Aai's Tiffin Service: A Pune Home Kitchen's Order Sheet

3. Personal finance and spending analysis

₹99 · Beginner · Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI

Twelve months of real-looking bank statements from 24 first-jobbers across five banks. Find where the salary goes, the forgotten subscriptions and the double charges.

You will practise: Parsing bank statement narrations; Subscription and recurring payment detection; Double charge detection; Savings rate dashboard.

Interview question it prepares you for: How did you turn messy bank narrations into merchants and categories?

See the project: Where Did My Salary Go: UPI and Bank Statements of 24 First-Jobbers

4. Onboarding funnel analysis

Free · Beginner · Python, SQL, Statistics

A free product analytics project: an onboarding funnel and an A/B test where the dashboard says the new screen lost. Find out what really happened.

You will practise: A/B test analysis with real pitfalls; Event data cleaning; Conversion by platform and source; Experiment readout for a product manager.

Interview question it prepares you for: What would you check before trusting an A/B test result?

See the project: The Funnel That Leaked: App Onboarding and an A/B Test

5. E-commerce discount and profitability analysis

₹249 · Hard · Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI

A year of messy order exports from a fictional Indian appliance brand selling on Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho and its own site: 14,701 order lines, 41 columns, 20 listing managers, 40 SKUs. Clean it, then settle the argument management can't: why the biggest sales month lost money, which discount depth stops paying, and why the customers who paid full price were worth several times more than the ones who came in on sale.

You will practise: Contribution margin by month and category; Returns, RTO and COD leakage; Marketing channel ROAS; Listing manager performance ranking.

Interview question it prepares you for: Why can the biggest revenue month lose money?

See the project: The Diwali Discount Trap: E-commerce Appliance Orders, India

6. Sales performance and incentive analysis

₹449 · Hard · Python, SQL

A year of hand-typed ERP entries from an 8-branch car dealer group in Delhi. Clean it, then settle the argument management can't: who really performed, what December's record month actually cost, and why the customers who refused last year's stock were the most profitable buyers of the year.

You will practise: Discounts and margin by month; Salesperson identity cleaning; Branch and model performance; Old stock vs new stock buyers.

Interview question it prepares you for: How do you clean salesperson names typed eight different ways?

See the project: The ₹176 Lakh Question: Tata Dealership Sales, Delhi

7. Last-mile delivery performance

₹249 · Hard · Python, SQL, Excel

A year of messy dispatch exports from a fictional regional courier serving the small cities of UP, Bihar, Jharkhand and MP: 14,560 shipments, 41 columns, 48 riders, 12 hubs, 6 shippers. Clean it, then settle the argument operations can't: why the on-time number is wrong, why a rider with perfect first attempts is the network's biggest problem, and which hub, which day and which parcel size are quietly costing the business its largest client.

You will practise: Measuring on-time delivery correctly; Rider and hub performance; COD vs prepaid failures and RTO; Profitability by weight, zone and service.

Interview question it prepares you for: How should on-time delivery be measured, and how can it be gamed?

See the project: Late Again: Last-Mile Delivery in Tier-2 India

8. Service turnaround time (TAT) analysis

₹249 · Hard · Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI

Rebuild the true turnaround time for 17,000 appliance service tickets and find whether the days go to the warehouse, the courier or the service centre.

You will practise: Spare parts delay root cause; Service centre performance ranking; Repeat complaint detection; Customer rating vs delay.

Interview question it prepares you for: How would you measure turnaround time honestly when tickets are closed early?

See the project: Spares Never Arrive: Appliance Service and Spare Parts TAT, India

9. Import shipment tracking

₹449 · Hard · Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI

Track 470 China import POs across six messy files: containers on water, overdue documents, pending payments and the cash plan, as of your own report date.

You will practise: Supplier delay analysis; Documents and payments chase list; Cash flow planning for imports; As-of-date reporting.

Interview question it prepares you for: How do you build a report as it looked on a past date?

See the project: Slow Boat from Ningbo: China Import Procurement Tracker

How to make any project stand out

  • Pick a domain you can talk about in an interview: retail, e-commerce, logistics or finance.
  • Show the cleaning, not just the charts. Recruiters want to see how you handled messy data.
  • End every project with a one page recommendation in plain words.
  • Publish the code on GitHub and the dashboard as screenshots or a link.

Questions

Where can I find datasets for these projects?

Each idea on this page links to a KOJOH project with its own messy dataset, tasks and a data dictionary. Kaggle and government open data portals are other good free sources.

Are these projects good for a portfolio?

Yes. Each one ends with cleaned data, answered business questions, a dashboard and a one page recommendation, which is what recruiters look for. Paid KOJOH projects give every buyer a personal brief, so your answers are your own.

Is the data real?

No. Every KOJOH dataset is synthetic, built to behave like real Indian business data, including the mess.
