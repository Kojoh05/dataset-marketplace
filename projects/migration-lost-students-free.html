What is messy in this dataset?

These are the problems you will find and fix before any analysis. They are put in on purpose, because real exports look like this.

  • The same student keyed as DJ/2025/0123 in one system and DJ-2025-123 in the other
  • The legacy roll number blank on about 6% of new-app records
  • Some students missing from the new app and some created twice
  • Money in rupees on one side and paise on the other, with one batch saved 100 times too small
  • Names in capitals on one side, title case (sometimes surname first) on the other

What business problem does this project solve?

A free SQL and data quality project: a coaching institute moved to a new app and the numbers stopped matching. Reconcile both systems, find every problem and write the checks.

Disha Classes coaches about 1,500 students for JEE, NEET and school foundation courses at three centres in Jaipur: Malviya Nagar, Vaishali Nagar and Mansarovar. For eight years every admission and fee receipt lived in an old desktop program in the Malviya Nagar office.

On the night of 2 September 2025, an outside vendor moved everything into a new cloud app: students, batches and every receipt since April. The director signed off the next morning because the screens looked fine.

By October the phones were ringing. Parents of scholarship students were told they owed fees they had already been let off. A few parents said their child was not in the app at all. The accountant says the fee collected from April to August is lower in the new app than in the old books, and nobody can say by how much or why.

You have both sides: the old system's students and receipts, the new app's students and payments, and the code map the vendor used. Your job is to reconcile them, find every problem the migration caused, put a number on each one, and write the checks that would have caught them on day one.

This is a first data engineering and data quality project: five files, real reconciliation work, and the kind of incident every data team meets sooner or later. Your license gives you your own centre and your own month to report on.

Frequently asked questions

Is this real company data?

No. Every row is synthetic: the company, the people and the transactions are invented. The data is built to behave like real business data, including the mess, so the project feels like real work.

What tools do I need?

SQL, Data Quality, Data Engineering work best, but the tools are your choice. What is checked is whether your answers are right.

How long does it take?

About 6 to 10 hours for most people, spread over a few evenings.

Can I put it on my portfolio?

Yes. Publish your analysis, code and dashboard on GitHub or LinkedIn. You can share up to 100 sample rows, but not the full files.
