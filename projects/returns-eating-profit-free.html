What is messy in this dataset?

These are the problems you will find and fix before any analysis. They are put in on purpose, because real exports look like this.

  • Dates month-first in the US file, day-first in the UK file and ISO or dd.mm.yyyy in the EU file
  • Prices as $48.99, £38.99, 45,99 € and 48.99 USD
  • 236 order lines exported twice
  • Countries as USA, U.S., GB, Deutschland, Holland; sizes as Small, SM, W30, EU 38
  • Returns with # order numbers, blank SKUs and 30 that match no order
  • Return reasons in free text: Runs small, changed my mind, Faulty

What business problem does this project solve?

A free beginner project: a year of an online fashion store's orders and returns from the US, UK and EU. Fix three date formats and three currencies, then find what returns really cost.

Thread & Thimble is a small online fashion brand: shirts, dresses, jeans, coats, shoes and a few accessories. It started in one country and now sells through three storefronts, one for the US (dollars), one for the UK (pounds) and one for the EU (euros).

Sales grew all year. Profit did not. The founder, Maya, thinks returns are the reason. Fashion shoppers order two sizes and send one back, a "free returns" promise costs the shop $8 every time, and some returned items come back damaged and cannot be sold again.

Nobody has ever put the numbers together, because the data is a mess. Each storefront exports dates its own way, so 03/04/2025 means 4 March in the US file and 3 April in the UK file. Prices come with $, £ and € signs, and the EU file uses commas for decimals. Countries, sizes and categories are spelled many ways. Some order lines were exported twice. The returns platform is a separate system, and its order numbers do not always match.

Maya has three questions. How much are returns really costing us? Which products, sizes and categories are the problem? And are our discount codes bringing in customers who just send things back?

This is a free first project for Excel, SQL and Power BI. Every row is synthetic, built to behave like real e-commerce data. Your licence gives you your own month and your own category to report on.

Frequently asked questions

Is this real company data?

No. Every row is synthetic: the company, the people and the transactions are invented. The data is built to behave like real business data, including the mess, so the project feels like real work.

What tools do I need?

Excel, SQL, Power BI work best, but the tools are your choice. What is checked is whether your answers are right.

How long does it take?

About 6 to 10 hours for most people, spread over a few evenings.
