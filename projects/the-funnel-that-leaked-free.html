The story

PaiseKhata is a savings app. To open an account, a new user has to get through six steps: open the app, start signup, verify the OTP, start KYC, finish KYC and make a first deposit. Most never make it. KYC is where the funnel leaks worst, so in February 2026 the product team tested a new video KYC screen (B) against the old document upload screen (A), with users split 50 / 50.

The dashboard says B lost. The product manager is ready to kill it. Before she does, she wants an analyst to look at the raw event export: 41,268 events from about 13,860 users.

The export has the usual problems. Android sends timestamps in UTC, iOS in India time, so some users seem to verify their OTP before they signed up. The app retries events and logs some twice. QA engineers tested on production with accounts that always convert. And the split does not look like 50 / 50 at all.

Clean the events, rebuild the funnel and read the experiment properly. Your license gives you an acquisition source and a city to look at closely.
