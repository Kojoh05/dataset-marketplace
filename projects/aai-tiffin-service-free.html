Aai's Tiffin Service: A Pune Home Kitchen's Order Sheet

The story

Aai (Mrs Kulkarni, but everyone calls her Aai) started cooking tiffins for three students in Kothrud in 2019. Today she sends lunch and dinner to offices in Hinjewadi and Baner, students in Kothrud and Viman Nagar, and a few families nearby. Four delivery boys, four plans, six days a week.

Every order goes into one shared Google Sheet. Aai types dates her way, her son Rohan types them his way, and Sneha, who helps in the evenings, has a third way. The same order sometimes gets typed twice. Plans are Veg Basic, Basic or VB, depending on who is typing. On 1 October 2025 Aai raised prices by ₹10 a meal, but Rohan kept typing the old prices for a few weeks.

Customers pay each month's bill by UPI or cash in the first weeks of the next month, and Aai writes it in a notebook (now typed up). Some pay late. A few left without paying.

Aai has three questions. Which plans and areas actually make money? Did the price hike drive customers away? And who owes her money, and how much?

This is a friendly first project for Excel and Power BI: three files, one clear story and the kind of questions a small business owner really asks. Your license gives you your own month and your own area to report on.

Questions

Is this real company data?

No. Every row is synthetic: the company, the people and the transactions are invented. The data is built to behave like real business data, including the mess, so the project feels like real work.

What tools do I need?

Excel, Power BI work best, but the tools are your choice. What is checked is whether your answers are right.

How long does it take?

About 6 to 10 hours for most people, spread over a few evenings.

Can I put it on my portfolio?

Yes. Publish your analysis, code and dashboard on GitHub or LinkedIn. You can share up to 100 sample rows, but not the full files.
