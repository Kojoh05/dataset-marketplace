Every KOJOH project is a business problem with the messy data that comes with it: an Indian kirana store's bills, a US-UK-EU fashion store's returns, a SaaS app's billing events, a courier's late deliveries, a lender's loan book. You clean the data, answer real questions in SQL or Python, build a dashboard in Power BI or Excel, and finish with something worth putting on your portfolio. Every row is synthetic, built to behave like the real thing.

How KOJOH projects work

Each project has four phases: clean the raw files, answer the business questions, build a two page dashboard, and write a one page note to the person who asked. Paid projects give every buyer a personal brief, so two portfolios never show the same answers. Submit your GitHub repo and LinkedIn post, and the KOJOH team reviews your work and issues a verified certificate.
