What is messy in this dataset?

These are the problems you will find and fix before any analysis. They are put in on purpose, because real exports look like this.

  • Item names typed by hand in several spellings each (case, spacing and short forms), matched to an item master
  • Loose items sold in mixed units: 0.5 kg, 500 gm, 2 pcs
  • Prices as text: 54, ₹54, Rs.54, 54.00
  • Three date formats and two time formats (24-hour and AM/PM)
  • About 2% of rows are exact duplicates (double-printed bills); returns carry negative quantities
  • Amount blank on about 5% of rows; customer names like Gupta ji and first names only

What business problem does this project solve?

A free first project: a year of real-looking bills from a Lucknow kirana store. Clean it, find what makes money and how much is stuck in udhaar.

Sharma General Store has sold milk, atta, biscuits and everything in between from the same corner in Aliganj for 22 years. Last April Sharma ji's nephew talked him into a ₹999 billing app on an old Android phone, so for the first time there is a record of every bill: 3,521 of them in a year.

The app was set up in a hurry. Two cashiers type item names however they like, loose atta goes in as 0.5 kg one day and 500 gm the next, some bills were printed twice, and the udhaar (credit) customers are written as Gupta ji, Sanjay bhaiya or just a first name. A separate notebook, now typed up, records who paid back what.

Sharma ji has three questions. Which items actually make him money, not just sales? When is the shop busiest? And how much is stuck in the khata, with whom?

This is a short, friendly first project: three small files, one clear story, and questions a real shop owner asks. Your license gives you your own month and your own category to report on.

What will you build, step by step?

  1. Phase 1, Python: Clean the Billing DataTyped names, loose quantities, double prints and returns into one clean table.Example task: Load the three files and profile them. Write down five problems you would hit if you summed the Amount column as it is.
  2. Phase 2, SQL: Answer Sharma ji's QuestionsProfit, busy hours, udhaar, Diwali, your month and your category.Example task: Revenue, profit and margin % for every month. Which month made the most profit, and is it the same as the month with the most revenue?
  3. Phase 3a, Dashboard: The ShopThe shop page.Example task: Build a one page shop dashboard: revenue, profit, bills and average bill for any date range, a monthly trend, top items, payment mode split, and filters for category and payment mode.
  4. Phase 3b, Dashboard: The Udhaar BookThe udhaar book.Example task: Build an udhaar tracker: every credit customer with what they owe, when they last paid and how long it has been, sorted by risk.
  5. Phase 4, DocumentationA one page note to the owner.Example task: Write a one page note to Sharma ji: what you cleaned, three findings he can act on next month, and what to do about the udhaar book. Use your category and your focus month as your worked examples.

Frequently asked questions

Is this real company data?

No. Every row is synthetic: the company, the people and the transactions are invented. The data is built to behave like real business data, including the mess, so the project feels like real work.

What tools do I need?

Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI work best, but the tools are your choice. What is checked is whether your answers are right.

How long does it take?

About 6 to 10 hours for most people, spread over a few evenings.

Can I put it on my portfolio?
